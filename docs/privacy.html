Untangle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Untangle is a free puzzle game for Android. This page explains what happens with your data when you use it. In short: nothing leaves your phone.

Data we collect

None. Untangle does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your phone so it can remember them between sessions: the chosen theme (light or dark), whether sound is muted, the current level, and the highest level you have unlocked. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Ads and in-app purchases

Untangle has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Internet and permissions

The app does not use the internet and requests no special permissions.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
